## Setup

- **Sponsor.** A long-running `mantle-aa sponsor serve` per phase, in the script's process, with
  the relayer key and the paymaster signer key from the environment (`relayerKey: ${…}`,
  `paymasterSignerKey: ${…}`). Its allowlist holds the phase's account; one SQLite database serves
  the whole run, so the daily caps apply across phases. Caps: `maxFeePerRequestMnt: "0.1"`,
  `dailyBudgetMnt: "1"`, `managementReserveMnt: "0.4"`, `accountDailyGas: 30000000`. The documented
  example cap of 0.05 MNT would refuse init with the first grant (`docs/DEFERRED_ISSUES.md`). On
  this run the relayer paid 0.041781 MNT for that transaction, and the bundler path's init cost
  the executor 0.044107 MNT.
- **Bundler.** `e2e/bundler/sepolia.sh up`/`down`, run by the script: Alto 0.0.21 in safe mode with
  the safe-validator patch, one executor, `debug_*` traced on a local anvil fork through
  `trace-proxy.mjs` (`docs/infra.md` §10.2).
- **Fixture and policy** as in `e2e/relayer/relayer.test.ts` and `e2e/bundler/path.test.ts`. The
  funder buys 7 mUSD of meme for the human before any session exists, then mints 200 mUSD to the
  account (principal by minting). Policy: `(MockUSD, approve)`, `(MockLaunchpad, buy)`,
  `(MockLaunchpad, sell)`; trusted spender MockLaunchpad; MockUSD total cap 100 mUSD and period cap
  50 mUSD; 24 h expiry; deadline window 600 s.
- **0 MNT, and the order of the phases.** The `relayer` and `bundler` phases never fund their
  human or agents. Each ends by checking that the account, trader and brief hold 0 MNT and that
  the account's EntryPoint deposit is 0; those checks also ran after steps 1 and 2. The value case
  and panic come after them, on their own fresh accounts. The value case's account is the only
  one that receives principal, and the panic root is the only one that pays gas. So the "0 MNT
  throughout" of steps 1–6 holds for the step 1–6 accounts. After the run, every agent address
  and the three delegated accounts still hold 0 MNT (checked with `cast balance`).
- **Expired key.** The chain's clock cannot be moved, so each path grants a second session,
  "brief", through the SDK with an expiry 10 minutes ahead (`MantleAccount.grantSession`; the CLI
  has no expiry option). `agent simulate` passes for it right after the grant, and the expiry
  item runs once the chain's time has passed it.
- **The public RPC** is load-balanced over nodes up to a block apart: `eth_blockNumber` went
  backwards in 2 of 25 consecutive calls. After every transaction the script waits until the head
  is two blocks past it, and it retries post-step state checks for up to 60 s.

## How each item is refused, and what is evidence of what

Every overreach of step 4 is refused three times, as in the fork suites:

1. **CLI:** `agent exec` refuses it with the semantic exit code (10 policy, 11 limit, 12 inventory,
   13 key expired or revoked). The script asserts that the submitter's nonce (the relayer, or Alto's
   executor) and the lane's EntryPoint nonce did not move.
2. **Sponsor:** the same calls, signed with the session key, go to the sponsor without the SDK's
   pre-check (`RelayerExecutor` / `BundlerExecutor`). The sponsor refuses with the reason in the
   tables, and nothing is submitted.
3. **On chain**, bypassing the CLI, the SDK, the sponsor and the bundler, as a real transaction
   from the funder with a fixed gas limit (no `eth_estimateGas`):
   - **Relayer path:** `execute(SignedBatchedCall, sig)`, signed by the session key with the funder
     as executor, sent straight to the account. The transaction is mined with status 0. Its revert
     reason is decoded from an `eth_call` of the same call from the funder at the latest block,
     made just before sending. A receipt carries no revert data, and the public RPC serves no
     `debug_traceTransaction`.
   - **Bundler path:** `EntryPoint.handleOps([op], funder)`, where the op is signed by the session
     key and its paymaster data by the paymaster's signer key (a paymaster that would sponsor
     anything). An op the hook stops in execution is included: the transaction succeeds, and
     `UserOperationEvent.success = false` with the hook's error in `UserOperationRevertReason`.
     Both are on chain, decoded from the receipt's logs ("op reverted: …"). A validation failure
     (`AA22`, `AA23`, `AA24`) reverts the whole transaction (status 0). Its `FailedOp` reason is
     decoded from the same call's `eth_call`.
   - The script checks before and after each item that the account's balances, allowance,
     remaining limit, inventory, MNT and EntryPoint deposit are unchanged.

Two items have no transaction, and say so:

- **ERC-1271:** `isValidSignature` is a view, and no CLI or sponsor path signs one. The session
  key's valid ERC-7739 signature is refused by the deployed account and hook in an `eth_call`
  against the live chain (`SessionKeySignatureRejected`).
- **Wrong paymaster, I10** (bundler path only; the relayer path has no paymaster): one mined
  `handleOps` transaction (`AA23 reverted: WrongPaymaster`), plus an `eth_call` with a state
  override that gives the account 1 MNT, which shows the hook, not an empty balance, refuses it.
  The strict bundler also refuses the same op (`-32500: AA23 reverted 0x084112ae`, the
  `WrongPaymaster()` selector).

### Panic (`panic`)

Setup: init with a grant (agent), and an agent buy, so the agent holds 5e21 meme of inventory. The
sponsor is then **stopped**. `account panic` (sponsored) fails, sending nothing (`Sponsor
unreachable`). `account panic --self-pay` with the root at 0 MNT also fails, sending nothing (the
root's nonce is unchanged). After the funder sends the root 0.05 MNT, `account panic --self-pay
--undelegate` completes in two phases:

1. One self-paid transaction from the root to itself, `execute(BatchedCall)`, which revokes the
   agent's key on Calibur, writes the hook tombstone and resets the MockUSD → launchpad
   allowance (`revokedKeys` = the agent's keyHash, `resetAllowances` = that one pair).
2. In a later block, a type-4 transaction delegating the EOA to the zero address. Afterwards the
   account has no code.

`account status` then shows `UNDELEGATED`, the agent's session `registered: false`, `{ active:
false, revoked: true }`, and the account's 5e21 meme fully reserved to it (frozen until the root
releases it). The root returned 0.041274 MNT to the funder; 0.000211 MNT stays on it. This is
the small panic. The worst case (8 keys, 128 allowance resets, ≈1.6 M gas self-paid) is measured
on a fork (`packages/cli/test/cli.panic.fork.test.ts`) and was not repeated here.

## Not run on Sepolia, and where it is covered

- **Embedded mode** (§8 "Also": E2E without a long-running sponsor). Every fork suite already runs
  §8 steps 1–6 twice, once against `sponsor serve` and once with `MANTLE_AA_EMBEDDED_RELAYER=1`:
  `e2e/relayer/relayer.test.ts` (b) and `e2e/bundler/path.test.ts` (b). The embedded sponsor
  runs the same sponsor code in the CLI's process. A second Sepolia pass would have spent about
  as much again (≈0.5 MNT) to re-prove the same code against the same contracts, so it was not
  repeated here.
- **Entry-point matrix** (§8 "Also"): direct `execute(BatchedCall)`, ERC-7821, SignedBatchedCall,
  `executeUserOp` and ERC-1271 against I1–I13 are covered by the contract tests
  (`contracts/test/`, `forge test -vvv`). This run exercised two of those entries on the real
  chain, SignedBatchedCall and `executeUserOp`, plus ERC-1271 by `eth_call`.
- **Wrong paymaster that is a second working, funded paymaster:** this needs a copy of the
  paymaster with its own deposit, which the fork suite builds with `anvil_setCode`
  (`e2e/bundler/path.test.ts`, I10). Deploying and funding one on Sepolia is not needed for
  §8: the hook's check is the same (`AA23 WrongPaymaster`) and is shown above with no paymaster.
- **Cross-path replay, cross-lane nonce consumption and a lapsed sponsorship window (`AA32`):**
  not §8 items; covered on the fork by `e2e/bundler/path.test.ts`.
